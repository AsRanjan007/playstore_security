# MusicVision Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 November 2026
**App:** MusicVision (Android package `com.ashutoshranjan.musicvision`; also the MusicVision iPhone app)
**Developer:** Ashutosh Ranjan
**Contact:** arj.nuz@gmail.com

MusicVision is an air instrument: the camera tracks your fingertips so you can play virtual strings and a tabla.
This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

- MusicVision has **no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own**.
- **Camera images never leave your device.** They are analysed in real time and then discarded. They are never saved, recorded or uploaded by MusicVision.
- Only your **app settings** are stored, and only on your device.

## Information the app uses

### Camera
- **Purpose:** the camera finds the position of your fingertips so you can play the instrument.
- **On your device only:** each video frame is processed in memory on the device (MediaPipe hand tracking on Android, Apple Vision on iPhone) and discarded immediately.
- **Not kept:** MusicVision does not store, record, transmit or share camera images or video.
- **Optional:** you can deny camera access and still play by touching the screen.

### Vibration
The app uses the vibration motor for touch feedback when you play. No data is involved.

### Settings stored on your device
- **What:** your choices, such as scale, key, layout, number of strings, instrument, play mode, finger mode, camera choice, sensitivity and haptics.
- **Where:** in the app's private storage on your device.
- **Removed:** when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
- **Not shared:** they are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

## Information we collect

MusicVision does not collect, sell or share personal information. The developer receives no data from the app.

## Third-party components

The Android app uses Google's **MediaPipe** library for on-device hand tracking.
- MediaPipe includes a Google component (`datatransport`) that declares internet access.
- That component may send Google limited, anonymous technical information about how the library performs, such as usage and diagnostics. It never sends camera images.
- Google handles this information under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app itself makes no network requests. The iPhone app uses only Apple's built-in frameworks.

If you install MusicVision from Google Play or the App Store, the store operator processes information about your download and device under its own privacy policy.

## Children

MusicVision does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app collects no personal information, so none is collected from children.

## Security

Camera frames are kept only in memory for the moment needed to track your fingertips. Settings stay in the app's private storage on your device.

## Your choices

- **Camera:** turn camera permission off at any time in your device settings.
- **Settings:** clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, to remove all stored settings.
